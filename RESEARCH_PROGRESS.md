# Research progress: 22 November 2026

**Author: Biswajit Jana**

Latest completed work: [Study 008: Independent validation](reports/010-validation-restores-identification.md).

- Derived simultaneous reference-to-validation drift bounds without treating a data-derived bound as fixed.
- Compared retaining channel-wise validation with collapsing it to a scalar envelope; proved the relevant feasible-set inclusion.
- Completed 29,000 synthetic experiments across fixed-cost allocations and transfer-mismatch stress cases, with 116,000 paired decisions.
- Replayed all count records, 2,320 decisions and 1,160 region-inclusion checks; reconstructed all rates, coverage summaries and fixed-bin histograms.
- Recovered useful power with direct validation while showing that unaccounted validation-to-science mismatch can destroy null control.
- Added three figures, a fixed-budget allocation derivation and a primary-source review of calibration products and optical distinguishability.

The solved subproblem is conditional statistical identification, not physical wormhole existence. [Study 006](reports/008-joint-calibration-identification.md) supplies the confidence construction and [Study 007](reports/009-calibration-transfer-drift.md) the drift-overlap obstruction. Validation adds information but does not establish its own transfer premise. Numerical beta quantiles remain outside validated arithmetic, and independent review remains pending.

## Next study specification

**Primary physical question:** can a second mode or complex phase impose cross-observable consistency that a credible alternative geometry cannot reproduce?

Derive the joint forward model before producing detection forecasts. Preserve source, clock and response nuisance parameters and build an actual physical comparator; rejecting a narrow generic barrier family cannot establish topology. Study 008 resolves the scalar-envelope versus channel-wise-validation question in its toy experiment, so merely repeating larger ideal counting runs is not the next physical advance.

**Instrument and statistical directions:** specify validation cadence and a justified response-dynamics bound; assess remaining residual-transfer ambiguity. The fixed-cost grid held reference M fixed and did not identify a confirmed global allocation optimum. Any adaptive allocation needs separate pilot and evaluation data. Validated inverse-beta arithmetic also remains unfinished.
