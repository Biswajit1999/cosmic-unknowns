## Next study specification

**Primary physical question:** which explicit action outside the excluded static minimal-scalar class could support a member of the family, and does the massive-probe distinction survive realistic nuisance parameters?

Massive scattering with two open channels is now implemented and checked. Next, profile or bound the unknown mass-scale product, frequency calibration and response-transfer parameters before making a detectability claim. A physical source/detector channel for a massive scalar must be specified; it is not automatically a neutrino or photon experiment. Exact thresholds and closed channels need additional numerical treatment. For supporting matter, select a concrete nonminimal or multi-field action, verify its background equations and then derive coupled perturbations. Merely assigning an arbitrary anisotropic stress does not solve the matter problem.

**Instrument and statistical directions:** specify validation cadence and a justified response-dynamics bound; assess remaining residual-transfer ambiguity. The fixed-cost grid held reference M fixed and did not identify a confirmed global allocation optimum. Any adaptive allocation needs separate pilot and evaluation data. Validated inverse-beta arithmetic also remains unfinished.
