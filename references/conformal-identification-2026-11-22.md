**Author: Biswajit Jana · 22 November 2026**

This review accompanies [Study 009](../reports/011-conformal-scattering-nonuniqueness.md). It is a targeted primary-source review, not a systematic priority search. No claim of unprecedented discovery is supported by the search. ## Unresolved literature work

Before a submission, trace the complete inverse-scattering and conformally related wormhole literature for this explicit family, including its alternative coordinates and field normalizations. Review exact matter-supported conformal metrics and distinguish massless scalar, Maxwell and coupled gravitational perturbations. Read the complete related reconstruction and transformation-optics papers before asserting a theorem-level comparison. A search hit, matching title or abstract is insufficient to establish either priority or physical equivalence.
