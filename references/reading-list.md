# Reading list and evidence log

Screened 8 November 2026. “Abstract” means title, authors, and abstract were inspected; it does not mean the derivation was verified. This is a selective seed bibliography, not a systematic literature review. Older foundational references must be followed through their subsequent literature.

| ID | Source | Use and inspection depth |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Morris & Thorne (1988), [Wormholes in spacetime and their use for interstellar travel](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.15620) | Foundational metric and traversability framework. Publisher metadata/summary screened; the local special-case algebra was derived and checked separately. |
| W2 | Gao, Jafferis & Wall, [Traversable Wormholes via a Double Trace Deformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.05687v3) | Abstract inspected. Coupled boundaries and negative averaged null energy in a specific holographic construction; does not imply an astronomical detection. |
| W3 | Maldacena, Milekhin & Popov, [Traversable wormholes in four dimensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04726v3) | Abstract and version history inspected. Magnetic construction with charged fermions; full validity conditions must be read before calculation. |
| W4 | Kontou (2024), [Wormhole restrictions from quantum energy inequalities](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.05963v2) | Abstract and selected sections inspected, including the smeared-null-energy discussion and assumptions. Not a verification of every bound. |
| W5 | Morris, Thorne & Yurtsever (1988), [Wormholes, time machines, and the weak energy condition](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/m644f-tbz27) | Institutional abstract inspected. A conditional causality argument, not evidence that time machines exist. |
| W6 | Freivogel, Fumagalli & Tomašević (2026), [How traversable is a traversable wormhole?](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.12528v2) | Abstract and discussion inspected, including assumptions about the second mouth and omitted field-theory terms. Preprint status; the calculations have not been reproduced here. |
| Q1 | Van Raamsdonk, [Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3035v2) | Abstract and metadata inspected. Model-based connection between entanglement and connected geometry, not a general experimental proof of emergent spacetime. |
| Q2 | Almheiri et al., [The entropy of Hawking radiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06872) | Abstract and journal reference inspected. Explains major progress on the information problem; prevents describing that field as having no progress. |
| C1 | Planck Collaboration, [Planck 2018 results. X. Constraints on inflation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06211) | Abstract screened. Foundational observational constraints, not a current exhaustive comparison of early-universe models. |
| C2 | [Big bang singularity resolution in quantum cosmology](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/acb752) | Publisher excerpt screened. Candidate reading about criteria for singularity resolution; full review pending. |
| C3 | Planck Collaboration, [Planck 2015 results. XVIII. Background geometry and topology of the Universe](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201525829) | Publisher excerpt screened. Searches for selected topologies and matched circles; constraints are not proof of simple connectivity. |
| X1 | Jafferis et al., [Traversable wormhole dynamics on a quantum processor](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05424-3), [authors' explanation](https://inqnet.caltech.edu/wormhole2022/) | Author project page inspected. Quantum circuit experiment and proposed gravitational interpretation, not creation of an astronomical tunnel. |
| X2 | Kobrin et al., [Comment on “Traversable wormhole dynamics on a quantum processor”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07897) | Search metadata screened. Required critical reading alongside the original paper and author replies before using this experiment. |

## First reading order

W1 → W4 → W3 → W6. Read W2 for the holographic connection, then Q1 and Q2 if quantum information becomes the chosen direction.

For every full-paper reading, record: precise claim; field content; spacetime and boundary conditions; approximation; matter source; energy bound; stability treatment; observable; failure modes; relevant critiques; and which calculation we can independently reproduce.

## Search scope

Initial searches covered traversable-wormhole quantum energy inequalities, four-dimensional magnetic wormholes, low-frequency transmission, emergent spacetime and entanglement, cosmic topology, and singularity-resolution criteria. Primary papers, author-hosted material, and collaboration publications were preferred. A citation-network search and comprehensive update to 2026 remain pending; no originality claim should be based on this list alone.
