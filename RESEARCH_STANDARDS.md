# Research standards

**Author: Biswajit Jana**

I distinguish established results under stated assumptions, observational inferences, published proposals, my reproductions, my conjectures and unresolved questions.

For each proposed idea I record the question, assumptions, equations, units, prior work, mathematical or observational output, and a result that would reject it. A mathematical metric alone does not establish a realizable source, stability, formation or observational existence.

I record source versions and reading depth. An abstract supports triage, not verification of a derivation. I check relevant corrections and errata and record which critiques or replies have actually been read. A literature search alone cannot establish originality.

I retain scripts, parameters, convergence checks, data provenance and figure-generation instructions. I separate illustrative geometry, simulated dynamics and observations. I do not present a visualization as evidence of physical existence.

I use computational and AI-assisted tools for drafting, literature exploration, algebra, code and criticism. They are not coauthors. The manuscript remains a working draft pending independent human and specialist verification; I do not present automated checks as peer review.

I credit original scientific results and respect source licenses. I do not redistribute paper figures or full text without checking permission. The repository remains private, with no publication or external submission implied by a push.
