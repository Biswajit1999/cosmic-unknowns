# Research progress: 22 November 2026

**Author: Biswajit Jana**

Latest completed work: [Study 009: Conformal scattering nonuniqueness](reports/011-conformal-scattering-nonuniqueness.md).

- Derived a family of regular conformal wormhole metrics with an identical massless-scalar radial operator for all angular modes and any fixed curvature coupling.
- Traced the cancellation to established harmonic conformal rescaling, with explicit limits on what can be inferred about full gravitational dynamics.
- Integrated the original field equation at 729 parameter combinations and compared 165 alternative numerical settings; added four scientific figures with raw data and hashes.
- Independently checked 12 four-dimensional tensors and 144 potential identities at 70-digit precision; replayed 41 production runs and 30 audit baselines.
- Found that small flux error does not certify phase accuracy for finite plane-wave boundaries in higher angular modes.
- Identified calibrated clocks, independently fixed distances and known massive probes as additional information outside the shared massless-scattering convention.

The new result is a conditional geometric nonuniqueness construction. Every member remains a wormhole and requires negative throat null-energy density under Einstein's equation. No supporting matter action, coupled stability or observational existence is established. Complex-amplitude equality assumes a shared tortoise coordinate and radiative normalization; calibrated areal distances can change the operational phase comparison. The known harmonic-rescaling mechanism is credited and novelty is not certified.

[Study 008](reports/010-validation-restores-identification.md) remains the latest statistical study: 29,000 synthetic experiments tested independent validation, fixed-cost allocations and transfer mismatch. [Study 006](reports/008-joint-calibration-identification.md) supplies the confidence construction and [Study 007](reports/009-calibration-transfer-drift.md) the drift-overlap obstruction. Validated inverse-beta arithmetic and independent specialist review remain unfinished.

## Next study specification

**Primary physical question:** can an explicit matter model sustain a member of the constructed family, and which physically calibrated measurement distinguishes its conformal parameter?

The previous proposed second-mode/phase test now has an exact counterexample within the specified massless-probe experiment. More counts or angular modes cannot remove that operator identity. Next, specify a matter action and verify its full equations before assessing coupled perturbative stability. In parallel scientific development, formulate a clock/distance measurement with source and detector nuisances, or implement a known-mass scalar solver with unequal asymptotic thresholds and appropriate long-range tails. The current massive-field figure is a potential difference only; it is not a massive scattering calculation. These are candidate next studies, not completed results.

**Instrument and statistical directions:** specify validation cadence and a justified response-dynamics bound; assess remaining residual-transfer ambiguity. The fixed-cost grid held reference M fixed and did not identify a confirmed global allocation optimum. Any adaptive allocation needs separate pilot and evaluation data. Validated inverse-beta arithmetic also remains unfinished.
