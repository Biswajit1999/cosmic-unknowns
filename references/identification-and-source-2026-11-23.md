The preceding [massive-probe review](massive-probes-2026-11-22.md) covers the scalar reduction and Coulomb-like long-range boundary conventions. The [conformal review](conformal-identification-2026-11-22.md) distinguishes a fixed-background harmonic wave identity from a symmetry of a full gravitational system.

The current source action is obtained by transforming a complete known action. This is different from asserting that a harmonic restriction alone generates a new symmetry. The transformed action retains the original phantom dynamics; apparent complexity in the Jordan coefficients is not evidence of new healthy matter.

No claim that the centered-parity parametrization, sensitivity identity or this explicit action is first in the literature is made.
