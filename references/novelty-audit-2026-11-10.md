# Originality and closest-prior-work audit

**Author: Biswajit Jana · 10 November 2026**

My aim is an original, useful research contribution. I cannot establish that nobody has ever done an idea through a finite literature search. The present evidence rules out calling all of this project new: scalar transmission, exact barrier scattering, wormhole imaging, spectral nonuniqueness and unknown classification channels have established literature.

Sources were followed to primary arXiv manuscripts, publisher records and the PMLR proceedings. Searches returning no exact configuration were treated as inconclusive. Index coverage, vocabulary differences, inaccessible papers, unpublished work and related results in other fields remain limitations. I did not perform an exhaustive citation census, multilingual search or proof of originality.

## Publishable claim boundary

A defensible working statement is: “I present a reproducible finite-band benchmark of scalar-transmission discrimination and construct bounded detector-error profiles that make a fitted alternative reproduce the selected Ellis probabilities.” Whether this combination warrants a novelty claim requires expert review and further comparison. A claim that the project proves physical wormhole existence, proves globally optimal calibration thresholds, or is entirely without precedent is unsupported.
